Here is a summary of how the **University Tutoring Scheduler** application works and the role of its core files:

---

### 💡 Core Concept & User Flow

1. **Authentication**: Users log in with their email.
2. **Schedule Creation (Owner Mode)**:
   - A tutor/user creates a scheduling space with available time slots and becomes the **Schedule Owner**.
   - The owner generates a **shareable link** (with a unique token/URL) to invite students/peers.
3. **Booking (Student / Attendee Mode)**:
   - Anyone with the link opens the booking view, selects an available slot, and submits a meeting request.
4. **Acceptance / Management**:
   - The Schedule Owner reviews incoming requests on their dashboard to **Accept** or **Reject** them.

### 🏆 Recommended Strategy: **Backend First ➔ Vertical Feature Slices**

Starting with the backend foundation allows you to define your data models and API contracts early. When you build the React frontend, you will connect real endpoints immediately rather than writing and discarding temporary mock data.
